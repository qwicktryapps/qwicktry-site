The Extension is free to try. Your free trial begins the first time you successfully press Start, not when you install it, and runs for the number of days stated in the Extension at that time. The standard trial is 14 days. We sometimes offer a longer trial during a launch period, and the length shown when your trial starts is the length you get. There is one trial per AtoZ account. The trial needs no payment details, and nothing is charged when it ends. Once it ends, continued use requires a valid pass.

3.2 What a pass is

A pass grants one person the right to use the Extension with one Amazon AtoZ account for a fixed period. It is not a subscription: it does not renew automatically and nothing is charged unless you buy a new pass. A pass simply expires at the end of its period.

A pass is tied to your AtoZ account rather than to a device, so it works on any computer or browser you sign in from. You may use it on your own devices without limit. If you buy a pass while your trial or an earlier pass is still running, the new days are added after it ends, so no days are lost.
